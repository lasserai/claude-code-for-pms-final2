# 02 · Super Hearing — prompts

**Context:** You joined Rook two weeks ago as PM on Dispatch.
Release 4.2 shipped on 12 August, just before you arrived, and
landed badly. Last session you built a context file and met the
company.

You still do not know what actually went wrong.

At the end of the session, ask Claude Code to save the prompts you
wrote yourself below — not the starter prompt. The closing slide has
the exact prompt to paste. By Module 6 this file is a prompt library
built from your own questions.

---

### 1.

How many customer interviews or incident tickets there are?

### 2.

Group the answers and describe the groups in one sentence

### 3.

Prioritize incident in three classes. What are the most urgent ones?

### 4.

What is the root cause for the most urgent Tier 1 incidents? Provide two alternative solutions with pros and cons and your recommendation.

### 5.

Which one of these groups are noise?

### 6.

Elaborate the not noise ones and put them in priority order in three tiers

### 7.

What are the not noise signals that are not stated in the incidents or interviews?

### 8.

How do you prioritize them that needs taken actions?

### 9.

CAn you show in one phrase the number 1 comments.

### 10.

What are the root cause problems in Tier 1 cases? And what are the options to solve them?

### 11.

Does the solution cover all the most urgent cases mentioned in tickets or interviews or there are not clearly stated anywhere?

### 12.

IS there anything else what can be done now for the second half.

### 13.

What are difference with this one previous one? What has been changed? Why?

### 14.

Based on this information what is the root cause analysis? What are the solution options for the Tier 1?

### 15.

As a product manager putting together the insights from interviews and tickets. Summarize the top 3 business problems worth solving.

### 16.

How to use the customer information right? Do you quantify first the groups and business problems worth solving by incident and the qualify them with the interviews?

### 17.

Do the summarize using the framework.

### 18.

Propose solutions for them

### 19.

If both piles are telling the truth, how can they both be right?
